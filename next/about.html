PrintFlow backups

A private tool of Central Cali 3D

PrintFlow is the app Central Cali 3D uses to run its 3D printing business: sales, stock, filament, purchases and expenses. It is a private business tool. It is not offered to the public, and it has no accounts for anyone outside the business.

"PrintFlow backups" is the part that, once the owner allows it, saves a nightly copy of the business's own records to a folder named CC3D-Backup in the owner's own Google Drive. It can only see and manage the backup files it creates there.

Read the privacy policy.
